Privacy

This page explains what 50/50 Cut (5050cut.com) collects and why. This is not legal advice.

Saved progress

High score, album, tokens, mute, and leaderboard nickname are stored in your browser's localStorage. There is no sign-in. Each device and each browser is its own account; switching phones or clearing site data makes a new account, and they cannot be merged.

Leaderboard

When you submit a score, the server uses the country of the IP (Cloudflare) to write that country's board and the global board. It stores a random device id, the nickname you set, an optional achievement title, the score, and the country code. You can change the nickname yourself, 2–12 characters. A title can only be the name of an achievement you have already unlocked. Do not enter a real name, phone number, or URL.

Ads

Production ads use Google web game ads (Ad Placement API). There is no prompt first. A rewarded video loads and plays only when you tap "Watch ad" in the shop, and tokens are given only after you finish watching. If no ad is available, you are told, and progress is not deducted.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings or My Ad Center. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices. See how Google uses advertising cookies.

Children

This game is for general casual players and is not designed specifically for children under 13. Do not let young children enter identifying information on the leaderboard.

Deletion

Clearing the browser's site data removes the local save. A leaderboard entry can be deleted by emailing jaydentay429@gmail.com with the random id and a request to remove it.
