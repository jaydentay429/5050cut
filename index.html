50/50 Cut

Swipe through the center of the object to cut it in half. If the middle is hard to judge, drag the pad on the right to turn the object, then cut.

A slice through the exact center scores 100. The farther the cut sits from the middle, the lower the score, and a cut too far off center scores nothing. Cuts of 85 or higher keep a combo going, and each extra cut in that combo is worth a little more. Fill the album as you go, and climb the board.
