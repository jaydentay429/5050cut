# Fruit stall assets

All files under `assets/models/fruit`, `assets/slices/fruit`, and `assets/env` are third-party works copied locally so the game never fetches them at runtime.

## 3D models

Playable GLBs under `assets/models/{world}/{id}/` are user-provided Tripo exports, Draco-compressed for the browser. They are **not CC0**. Do not ship unauthorized Sketchfab downloads.

Tripo plan at export (owner report, 2026-09-25):

- **Paid (commercial use per current Tripo paid-plan terms):** all playable models, including apple and banana re-exported 2026-09-25.
- ~~Free: apple, banana.~~ Replaced with paid exports (`apple 3d model.glb`, `banana 3d model.glb`).

Keep the paid-plan invoice / dashboard screenshot with the export dates. Fruit stall: `assets/models/fruit`. The other nine worlds: `assets/models/{veg,flower,pastry,candy,stationery,kitchen,night,toys,mineral}`.
